  <h2 class="md:text-2xl text-xl font-medium title-font text-text-dark mt-10 mb-4">Where names appear today</h2>

  <p>
    Every document page records the people the project has identified in that document:
  </p>

  <ul class="list-disc list-inside">
    <li><span class="font-bold text-text-dark">Primary parties</span> — the people the document is chiefly concerned with, such as the testator of a will or the buyer and seller in a bill of sale.</li>
    <li><span class="font-bold text-text-dark">Scribe</span> — the <em>escribano</em> who wrote the document.</li>
    <li><span class="font-bold text-text-dark">Witnesses</span> — those present when the document was made, often the <em>alcaldes</em>, <em>regidores</em>, and other officers of the town.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    Names are given as they were recorded, so the same person can appear with different spellings from one
    document to the next, and many people appear with a Spanish baptismal name alongside a Zapotec one.
  </p>

  <h2 class="md:text-2xl text-xl font-medium title-font text-text-dark mt-10 mb-4">Looking for a particular person</h2>

  <p>
    A person index that gathers every appearance of a name across the collection, and that reconciles
    variant spellings, is still being built. In the meantime, a name that appears in the body of a
    transcribed document can be found by searching for it in
    <a href="/explore/documents">All Documents</a> — choose <span class="font-mono">Transcription</span>
    in the <span class="font-mono">Search Within</span> menu to search transcribed text alone, or leave the
    menu on <span class="font-mono">All Fields</span> to search titles, towns, and archives at the same time.
  </p>

  <p>
    Because the documents of a single town tend to name the same families and officials over time,
    <a href="/explore/towns">browsing by town</a> is often the quickest way to find related people.
  </p>

  <p>
    If you are researching a particular family or individual and would like to know what is held here,
    <a href="https://ticha.haverford.edu/en/form/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">get in touch with the Ticha team ↗</a>.
  </p>
